Step 1: Select Country ➔ Step 2: Select State / Region ➔ Step 3: Search Bus Number

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Trip AI.

How do I place a Google Lens scan rectangle on the map?

Simply double-click anywhere on the 4K satellite map. A resizable and draggable rectangle box will spawn at that exact spot. You can drag it by its header bar or resize it using any of the 8 edge/corner handles.

Are my saved area notes and itineraries private?

Yes! All saved inspection notes are stored directly in your browser's private session cookies (trip_lens_notes) and local storage. No personal data is sent to external advertising servers. You can click "Delete Cookies" anytime to erase everything.

Can I use this app on my smartphone without lag?

Yes! The entire interface is responsive and lightweight with mobile-optimized button sizes, zero-overlap floating controls, and hardware-accelerated map rendering. For a dedicated ad-free commercial license, click the WhatsApp button (+91-7903933705).

Why do I need to select Country and State before searching for a bus number?

Public bus line codes often overlap internationally (e.g. Route 72 runs in Paris, London, and New York; AC-5 in Jaipur; KIA-8 in Bengaluru; E100 in Dubai). Selecting your Country and State / Region first guarantees that Trip AI matches the exact local transport authority, stops sequence, regional currency fare, and generates a pinpoint Google Maps transit route.

How does the live GPS Day Tour Planner calculate routes?

When you share your GPS coordinates, Trip AI calculates the geodesic distance from your current location to all selected landmarks and runs optimization algorithms to generate three distinct plans: Shortest Distance, Cheapest Public Transit, and Balanced Sightseeing.

How does the Mobile Quick-Jump bar and Navigation menu work?

On mobile devices, you have three quick ways to navigate:
1. Quick-Jump Pill Bar: Swipe horizontally across the sticky pill bar at the top to jump directly to Quick Start, Google Lens, GPS Planner, Bus Finder, Food Radar, or FAQ.
2. Mobile Menu Drawer: Tap the top-right menu button (☰) to open a full categorized drawer with icon cards.
3. Bottom Quick Dock: Tap bottom icons anytime for 1-click access to App, Sections, WhatsApp Buy, Theme Switcher, or Back to Top.

How does the automatic virtual keyboard scrolling work when typing on mobile?

When you tap any search input, destination box, or Google Lens question field on your smartphone, Trip AI's Virtual Keyboard Auto-Scroll Engine detects the virtual keyboard opening and smoothly shifts the screen upward. The active typing box remains centered and visible above the keyboard throughout your session.
