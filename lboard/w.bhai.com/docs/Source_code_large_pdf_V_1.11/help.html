⚡ 60-Second Quickstart

Get Running Instantly

Follow these 5 core steps to build your first professional diagram or annotated lecture in under a minute.

1

Choose Your Canvas Tool

Select the Select tool (V) to move items, Pan tool (H) to glide across the infinite space, or choose Pen, Pencil, Marker to draw freehand.

2

Drop Shapes, Notes or PDF Slides

Click any shape from the left Design Library (Rectangle, Pill, Circle, Diamond) or simply drag and drop a PDF/Image file directly onto the canvas.

3

Connect with Smart Arrows

Hover near any box or hold Shift to reveal magnetic edge handles. Drag to another box — Lumenboard auto-routes with straight, elbow, or curved lines with bridge bumps!

4

Style & Organize in Lessons

Use the floating Inspector to customize tone, fill opacity, borders, and rotation. Add multiple slides using the bottom Lesson Bar.

5

Export Everywhere

Click Export in the topbar to download a standalone Interactive HTML, multi-page PDF, PowerPoint PPTX, or full ZIP archive.

🧭 The Lumenboard Workspace Tour

Layout & Architecture

Lumenboard is designed for distraction-free flow with an ultra-responsive 4-zone workspace.

👑

1. Top Navigation Bar

Contains the File Menu (Import PDF/Image/GIF/JSON, Clear Storage), project status auto-save indicator, Theme Switcher (Dark/Light), and the master Export Center button.

📐

2. Left Tool Rail & Library

Switch between Design Library (shapes, arrows, technology icons), Templates, Batch Delete Pages, Text Visualizer, and Code Visualizer.

🎨

3. Canvas Stage & Floating Toolbar

The heart of your workspace: dynamic expandable canvas (up to 8000×6000px), floating drawing options, undo/redo, rich-text styling toolbar, table picker, zoom controls, and Fullscreen toggle.

🎛️

4. Inspector Panel & Bottom Lessons

Live Inspector for deep property styling (colors, stroke widths, tone palettes, layer depth) and the multi-lesson slide tab navigator for organizing multi-page presentations.

💡
Pro Tip: Dual-Layer Offline Storage
Lumenboard saves your canvas instantly to LocalStorage and stores heavy media (videos, PDFs, animated GIFs) in high-capacity browser IndexedDB. Your work stays private, offline, and never leaves your machine!

🔄 Smart Connectors & Line Routing

Intelligent Flow Links

Lumenboard takes the pain out of diagram lines with smart collision detection, bridge bumps, and flexible elbow routing.

4 Connector Styles

  • Straight: Direct point-to-point connection.
  • Elbow (Orthogonal): Clean 90-degree circuit-board routing.
  • Curved (Bézier): Elegant flowing curves.
  • Dashed: Async, optional, or dependency linkages.

Bridge Bumps & Snapping

When two connection lines cross, Lumenboard automatically renders an arching Bridge Bump to make the flow clear and readable without visual tangling.

Hold Shift while hovering over any shape to see magnetic connection handles.

💡 Pro Workflows & Step-by-Step Recipes

Mastery Blueprints
📐 Recipe 1: Building a Microservices Architecture Diagram in 2 Minutes ▼
  1. Open the left Design Library and drag 3 rectangles onto the canvas: API Gateway, Auth Service, and Orders Service.
  2. Drag a Pill shape for Client Browser and a Database Icon from the Desktop Icon Grid for PostgreSQL.
  3. Hold Shift and drag from the API Gateway edge handle to the services to create Elbow Connectors.
  4. Select the entire cluster, drag a Frame over them, and label the frame "Production VPC".
  5. Click Export → Scalable SVG to embed in your documentation!
📑 Recipe 2: Annotating a Lecture PDF & Delivering a Live Class ▼
  1. Drag and drop your course slides PDF onto the Lumenboard canvas.
  2. Lumenboard creates one lesson per slide automatically.
  3. Hit Ctrl+F to enter clean Fullscreen Mode.
  4. Use the Marker tool to highlight key formulas and the Pen tool to write live explanations.
  5. At the end of class, click Export → Lesson-by-Lesson PDF to distribute annotated slides to students!
🪄 Recipe 3: Generating Flowcharts from Markdown Notes ▼
  1. Click Text Visualizer from the left rail.
  2. Paste your meeting minutes or bulleted process steps.
  3. Select HLD (Concept Overview) and click Generate Diagram.
  4. Lumenboard arranges the cards with arrows and styled tones automatically!

❓ Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Support Center
Where is my data stored? Is it secure? ▼
All your diagrams, notes, PDFs, and media assets are stored 100% locally in your browser using LocalStorage and IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to external third-party cloud servers without your explicit action.
How do I clear browser storage or reset the application? ▼
Click the File Menu in the top-left corner and select Remove Browser Saved Data. This cleans up LocalStorage and resets the IndexedDB media database after asking for confirmation.
Why did a large video take a few seconds to load? ▼
Lumenboard includes a built-in WebAssembly FFmpeg converter. If you drop an AVI or non-native video format, it automatically transcodes the video to web-optimized WebM format entirely inside your browser for seamless playback.
How can I share my diagram with a colleague who doesn't have the app? ▼
Use Export → Lesson HTML. It outputs a single, self-contained HTML file that opens in any browser on Windows, Mac, iOS, or Android with zero installation required!
