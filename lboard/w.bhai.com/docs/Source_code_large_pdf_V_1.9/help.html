✨ Overview & Core Philosophy

Why Lumenboard is built differently from ordinary diagramming tools.

Lumenboard bridges the gap between raw, tactile whiteboard brainstorming and precision technical architecture diagrams. Rather than forcing you into rigid vector boxes or messy bitmap paint programs, Lumenboard gives you an infinite multi-lesson workspace where freehand pen sketches live seamlessly alongside snap-to-edge connectors, live-updating code flows, animated GIFs, and formatted notes.

🎯

Tactile Whiteboard + Precision Vectors

Chapter 2

🚀 5-Minute Quickstart Guide

Get your first interactive diagram and multi-page lesson ready in minutes.

1

Create or Drop Content onto the Canvas

Click □ on the left tool rail to add a concept card, or simply drag and drop any image, GIF, or PDF document directly from your desktop onto the workspace. Use T to place formatted text.

2

Connect Objects with Interactive Edge Handles

Hover near the edge of any card or image to see the round + connection handle. Hold Shift and drag a line to another shape to link them with automatic smart routing and bridge jumps!

3

Sketch & Highlight with Pen & Marker

Select the Pen tool (✒) or Marker tool (▰) to annotate, underline, or circle important elements with high-fidelity pressure curves and translucent highlighter strokes.

4

Publish Anywhere in 1 Click

Hit Export ↗ in the top-right corner. Choose Lesson HTML for a standalone interactive slide deck with mobile touch gestures, PowerPoint PPTX for native vector slides, or Lesson-by-Lesson PDF.

💡
Pro-Tip: Instant Local Save
Press Ctrl + S at any moment to save your entire workspace into browser storage. When you return, Lumenboard restores your exact lessons, layout, and active state!

Chapter 6

↗️ Smart Connectors, Routing & 3D Bridge Jumps

Intelligent line routing that automatically keeps complex diagrams clean and readable.

⚡

Instant Edge Handle Wiring

Hover over any card, decision box, or image to reveal edge + handles. Hold Shift and drag directly to another box. A popup style chooser lets you pick between Straight (→), Orthogonal Elbow (↳), Curved (∿), or Dashed (⇢) connectors instantly!

🌉

Automatic 3D Bridge Jumps (Arc Bumps)

When two connector lines cross on the canvas, Lumenboard automatically calculates the exact intersection coordinate and renders a beautiful curved 3D bridge jump (arc bump) over the underlying line. No more tangled, ambiguous spaghetti diagrams!

❓ Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions and performance optimizations.

Where are my diagrams stored? Is there any risk of cloud data leak? ▼
Lumenboard operates on a 100% local-first architecture. All diagram nodes, freehand strokes, and lesson tabs are stored strictly inside your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage when you press Ctrl+S. No diagram content is ever transmitted to remote cloud servers.
How do I export to Microsoft PowerPoint without broken lines? ▼
Click Export ↗ > PowerPoint PPTX. Lumenboard's custom export engine translates frames, cards, connectors, and arrowheads into native Microsoft Office vector shapes. If you need freehand handwriting in your slides, export as Scalable SVG or High-Resolution PNG.
How can I handle 100+ lesson pages smoothly? ▼
Lumenboard features lazy virtualization for large multi-page decks. Use the Delete pages tab in the sidebar with 100-page batch range filters (Select 1–100) and tokenized exact search to manage large curricula effortlessly.
How do I clear browser saved data if I want a completely fresh project? ▼
Open the File Menu in the top navigation bar and select Remove browser saved data. This resets all local storage caches and IndexedDB media safely.
