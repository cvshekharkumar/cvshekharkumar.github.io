STEP-BY-STEP

60-Second Quickstart

Get from zero to an interactive multi-lesson diagram in four intuitive steps.

1

Choose a Shape or Drawing Tool

Select a tool from the left vertical rail. Click □ Concept, ◇ Decision, or press T to place a rich formatted text box. For freehand notes, switch to ✒ Pen, ✎ Pencil, or ▰ Marker.

2

Connect Nodes with Smart Directional Arrows

Hover over any shape's edge until the purple + connector handle appears. Drag from one shape to another—Lumenboard will automatically attach an arrow. If two arrow lines cross, Lumenboard creates an automatic Bridge Bump so lines never look confusing!

3

Customize Colors, Typography & Layering

Click any element to open the right-side Properties Inspector. Tune fill colors, toggle transparency, adjust rotation (with 15° snap using Shift), insert multi-column tables, or upload custom icons.

4

Export & Share in Any Format

Click the top right Export ↗ button to download high-resolution multi-page PDF documents, scalable vector SVGs, standalone interactive Lesson HTML files (with touch-swipe support), or structured Website ZIP bundles.

Smart Connectors & Automatic Bridge Jumps

6 Versatile Arrow Styles

HELP & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Quick answers to common questions and technical tips.

Is my diagram data sent to external cloud servers? ▼
No! Lumenboard operates 100% client-side inside your web browser. All diagram data, PDF processing, video conversions, and text parsing happen locally on your device for absolute privacy and security.
How do I export all lessons into a single PDF document? ▼
Click the top right Export ↗ button and select Lesson-by-lesson PDF. Lumenboard will sequentially render each lesson canvas and combine them into a single, polished multi-page PDF document.
How do I clear saved browser data or reset my canvas? ▼
Open the File menu at the top left and select Remove browser saved data. This safely erases your locally cached project data and resets the studio to a fresh workspace.
How can I reorder or organize multiple lesson pages? ▼
You can simply click and drag any lesson tab at the bottom of the screen to move it into your desired position. You can also right-click any tab to rename it or assign a unique color badge.
Why are my arrow connectors jumping over each other? ▼
This is Lumenboard's signature Smart Bridge Bump feature! When two connector lines cross on different layers, an automatic curved arch is rendered over the intersecting line to prevent visual confusion and keep schematics clean.
