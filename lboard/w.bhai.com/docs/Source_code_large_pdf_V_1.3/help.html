60-Second Quickstart Guide

Follow these five rapid steps to create your first professional connected diagram or annotated lesson deck.

1

Add Shapes or Concept Cards

Click any shape in the left Design panel (Rectangle, Circle, Diamond, Start/End Pill) or use the quick buttons on the left rail (□ Concept, ◇ Decision, ⌗ Frame). Double-click the placed shape to rename it or add rich notes.

Pro-tip: Click anywhere on the white canvas to set the insertion cursor, then click a shape button to insert it precisely at that location.
2

Connect Shapes with Smart Arrows

Hover over any shape and grab the circular + connector dot, or hold Shift and drag from any edge to a target shape. Release to reveal the Arrow Style Picker (Straight, Elbow 90°, Curved Bézier, or Dashed).

Dynamic Bridge Feature: When arrows cross each other on different layers, Lumenboard automatically renders a stylish 3D arch jump-over bump so lines never look tangled!
3

Annotate with Pencil, Pen, or Marker

Switch to the Pencil (✎), Pen (✒), or Marker Highlighter (▰) on the left rail. Choose your color and stroke thickness from the top canvas toolbar to sketch formulas, underline key points, or highlight text.

4

Manage Multi-Page Lessons

Organize multi-step workflows using the bottom Lesson Tab Bar. Click ＋ to add new lesson pages, double-click a tab to rename it, drag tabs to reorder, and right-click to apply custom tab colors.

5

Export & Deliver

Click the top-right Export ↗ button. Choose Lesson-by-Lesson PDF (renders each lesson tab as a high-definition PDF page), High-Resolution PNG (crisp 2x retina graphic), Scalable SVG, or Lesson ZIP.

Smart Connectors & Dynamic Jump Bridges

Arrows in Lumenboard are fully dynamic. They automatically re-route, adjust endpoints, support custom curve waypoints, and feature automated 3D jump-over bridges.

→
Straight Arrow & Line
Direct point-to-point connections. Toggle between single arrowhead (→), double arrowhead (↔), or plain line (―).
↳
Elbow (90° Orthogonal)
Right-angled stepped connector. Ideal for electrical schematics, database entity-relationship diagrams, and structured software architecture.
∿
Curved Bézier Spline
Smooth, organic curve with adjustable inflection point. Gives flowcharts a modern, handcrafted look.
⇢
Dashed / Dotted Connector
Dashed line representation for asynchronous events, optional dependencies, network streams, or secondary relationships.
🌉
Dynamic Jump Bridge (Connector Bumps) Technology

In traditional diagram tools, intersecting lines create confusion. Lumenboard automatically calculates line segment intersections in real time. If two connectors cross each other on different z-layers, the top connector smoothly arches over the lower connector with an elevated 3D jump bridge arch!
