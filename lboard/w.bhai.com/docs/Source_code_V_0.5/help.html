Workspace
🔍

Precision Zooming

Scale smoothly from 35% to 200%.

  • Use the − and ＋ buttons in the footer.
  • Hold Ctrl / Cmd and scroll your mouse wheel.
  • Click the ⌗ Fit Diagram button to center and frame all objects automatically.
✥

Fluid Pan Tool

Effortlessly glide across expansive architectures.

  • Activate the Pan Tool (✥) in the left rail.
  • Middle-click anywhere and drag your mouse.
  • Preserves viewport focus even when zooming into intricate details.

Customize the active canvas resolution up to 8000 × 6000 px.

  • − / ＋ Controls: Expand or shrink working bounds by 400×300px chunks.

💡 Pro Tip: Fullscreen Mode

Press Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on macOS) or click the ⛶ Fullscreen icon in the top header to maximize your diagram onto the full display. Lumenboard automatically handles file-picker dialogs and seamlessly restores fullscreen mode.

Inking Engine

  • ✒ Pen: Solid, crisp vector ink for diagrams, math equations, and handwriting.

↳ Smart Connectors & Wire-Jump Bridges

Automated orthogonal pathfinding, collision avoidance, and wire-crossing bridge arcs.

Pathfinding
↗

Shift + Edge Drag Connection

Connecting two shapes takes under 1 second:

  1. Hold Shift and hover near any shape or arrow endpoint.
  2. A green + handle appears on the nearest border edge.
  3. Drag the dashed purple line to your target shape and release.
  4. Select your preferred arrow style (Straight, Elbow, Curved, or Dashed) from the pop-up menu!
∿

A* Routing & Jump-Over Bumps

  • Obstacle Avoidance: Orthogonal elbow connectors automatically navigate right-angle paths around intermediate cards.
  • Smart Jump Bridges: When two connector lines cross, the upper line automatically creates a clean wire-jump bridge arc so schematics never look tangled!
  • Manual Waypoints: Drag orange bend handles to customize line paths, or click "Reset automatic route" in the Inspector.

Delete Selection Delete / Backspace Deletes all selected cards, lines, notes, or strokes
Toggle Fullscreen Ctrl + F / Cmd + F Enters or exits distraction-free fullscreen presentation mode
