60-Second Quick Start

Get from zero to your first professional diagram in 4 effortless steps.

Beginner Friendly
1

Pick a Shape or Tool

Select the Rectangle (□), Decision (◇), or Text (T) tool from the left rail or drag templates from the left sidebar panel.

2

Draw & Connect

Hover near the border of any shape to reveal circular connection ports. Drag an arrow to another box — it snaps automatically with smart obstacle avoidance.

3

Annotate & Sketch

Switch to the Pen (✒) or Marker (▰) tool to write freehand notes, highlight key concepts, or sketch ideas on your hardware stylus tablet.

4

Export & Present

Click Export ↗ to save your entire multi-lesson deck as an editable PowerPoint (.pptx), high-res PDF, or crisp PNG image.

💡
Zero Configuration Needed

Lumenboard automatically saves all your changes in your browser's persistent local storage. You can close your tab anytime and pick up right where you left off!

Pro Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common scenarios and power user advice.

How does Lumenboard store massive 3GB PDFs without running out of browser memory?
Lumenboard utilizes browser IndexedDB media blob storage paired with range-based chunk streaming. Pages are rendered into crisp PNG raster assets on demand and indexed locally, keeping memory usage ultra-low even across 100+ page documents.
Can I edit my diagram directly via JSON?
Yes! Right-click on any lesson tab at the bottom and choose "Edit Lesson JSON". You can inspect or modify the underlying coordinates, shapes, tone colors, and connection endpoints directly.
How do I adjust connector bridge height or route points manually?
Simply click on any connector line to select it. Drag the center circular handle to create a custom bend point or route anchor. When multiple connectors overlap, bridge arcs are calculated automatically.
Where can I download sample templates?
You can download sample lesson packs anytime from the top File menu: Download sample ZIP (learner.zip) or Download DOCX template (Doc_to_diagram.docx).
