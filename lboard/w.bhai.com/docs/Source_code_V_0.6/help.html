Master Lumenboard in Minutes

60-Second Quick Start

Go from a blank slate to a presentation-ready interactive diagram in five effortless steps.

1

Choose a Template or Start Fresh

Click Templates in the top menu or the left drawer tab to load a structured starting point (Architecture, Algorithm Flowchart, Web Stack, or Concept Map), or use the default clean whiteboard.

2

Drop Concepts, Shapes & Tech Icons

Click any item in the Design Library (Rectangles, Start/End Pills, Decision Diamonds, Sticky Notes, or Desktop Icons like Browser, Database, Cloud) to instantly place it on your active canvas cursor.

3

Connect Nodes with Smart Auto-Routing

Hover near any box edge, hold Shift, and drag the purple + handle directly to another box. Pick from Straight, Orthogonal Elbow, Curved Bezier, or Dashed styles.

4

Annotate with Freehand Stylus & Rich Text

Switch to the Pencil, Pen, or Marker Highlighter on the left rail for fluid handwriting and diagram markup. Double-click any text card to format words or insert 8×8 tables.

5

Export to PowerPoint, SVG, PNG or ZIP

Click the top-right Export ↗ button. Choose Editable PowerPoint for native shapes, Scalable SVG for publications, or Lesson ZIP for multi-slide archiving.

💡
Pro Tip: Paste Screenshots Instantly

Copy any screenshot or image to your clipboard and press Ctrl + V anywhere on the canvas. It will be immediately embedded into your diagram as a moveable asset!

Text & Lecture Notes Visualizer

Convert raw paragraphs, textbook chapters, and meeting transcripts into connected process maps.

How to Use Text Visualization:
  1. Click Visualize text in the top menu or the TXT icon on the left rail.
  2. Select Concept overview (HLD) for big-picture conceptual groupings or Detailed flow (LLD) for sequential step-by-step procedures.
  3. Paste your raw text (e.g. Biology lesson, Business onboarding flow, CI/CD deployment guide).
  4. Click Generate diagram →. Lumenboard will extract key concepts, format cards, and connect them in sequential reading order automatically.

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions and technical optimizations.

Are my diagrams and code snippets saved securely? ▼
Yes. Lumenboard operates with a client-first privacy architecture. All active diagram data is automatically synchronized to your local browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage). No diagram content, text notes, or code ASTs are ever sent to remote cloud servers without your explicit action.
Why are exported PowerPoint (.pptx) shapes editable? ▼
Unlike other tools that simply paste a flat screenshot into a PowerPoint slide, Lumenboard translates each diagram card, decision diamond, connector arrow, and text label into native OpenXML PowerPoint vector shape primitives. You can change colors, resize shapes, and retype text directly in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides.
How do I configure my Huion or Wacom graphic tablet for drawing? ▼
Simply plug in your tablet and select the Pen or Pencil tool from the left rail. Lumenboard utilizes the standard W3C PointerEvents API with pressure support. If your stylus barrel button is mapped to right-click, Lumenboard automatically prevents context menu popups while in drawing mode so your strokes are never interrupted.
How do I adjust the white canvas boundary dimensions? ▼
At the bottom-right footer of the studio, use the − and ＋ buttons next to the dimension indicator (e.g. 2400 × 1600) to expand or contract the working canvas area. You can also click Fit to automatically wrap the white stage boundaries snuggly around your diagram items.
How can I import an existing lesson pack or DOCX curriculum? ▼
Click the File menu in the top bar and select Import DOCX lesson pack. You can also download the sample Doc_to_diagram.docx template from the File menu to see the exact structured heading and bullet format recognized by the parser.
