● GETTING STARTED

5-Minute End-to-End Walkthrough

Follow these 6 simple steps to create, link, sketch, and export your first professional visual lesson.

1

Create or Select a Lesson Frame

Click ⌗ Frame or drag a frame from the left toolbar. Double-click to title it (e.g., "Web Foundations: Client to Server"). Frames act as parent containers that move all internal children together.

Tip Frames carry all contained nodes when dragged or duplicated!
2

Add Concepts, Shapes & Desktop Icons

From the left rail or Design Library drawer, click Rectangle, Circle, Decision, or Desktop Icons (like WEB, DB, API, </>). Double-click any node to customize title and description.

3

Connect Nodes with the Shift-Drag Gesture

Hover near the edge of any box, press and hold Shift until a blue + handle appears, then drag to your target node. Release to choose Straight, Elbow (Smart Routing), Curved, or Dashed arrow!

Shortcut Hold Shift + Drag from edge = Instant Smart Connection
4

Annotate with Rich Text & Freehand Drawing

Use the T Text tool to type rich notes with bold, italic, custom colors, bullet lists, and tables. Switch to ✎ Pencil, ✒ Pen, or ▰ Marker Highlighter to hand-draw annotations.

5

Create Multiple Lessons in One Project

Click the ＋ Add Lesson button at the bottom bar. Double-click tabs to rename them, and right-click to apply color badges. Switch seamlessly between slides with independent undo/redo histories.

6

Export Everywhere in One Click

Click Export ↗ in the top-right corner. Choose Editable PowerPoint (.pptx) for presentation slides, Scalable SVG, High-Res PNG, or Lesson-by-Lesson PDF.

● INTELLIGENT ARROWS

Smart Connectors & Obstacle-Avoiding Routing

Lumenboard features an advanced graph routing engine that connects nodes without manual line adjustments.

↳ Obstacle-Avoiding Elbow Arrows

When connecting two nodes, the algorithm scans for intermediate boxes on the canvas and routes through free corridors to avoid unsightly line-crossing collisions.

∿ Smooth Bezier Curved Arrows

Provides fluid, organic relationships between distant concepts, perfect for cycles, feedback loops, and process returns.

⇢ Dashed Connectors

Ideal for async operations, optional steps, loose coupling, or background message queues.

↔ Bidirectional & Plain Lines

Switch between single arrowheads, bidirectional dual heads, or clean border lines via the Properties Inspector.

PRO GESTURE: INSTANT CONNECTION WORKFLOW Keyboard + Pointer
1. Hover your cursor over the perimeter edge of any node. 2. Hold down the [Shift] key — a circular (+) handle appears on the hovered edge. 3. Drag the line towards your target node. 4. Release the mouse button — a popup menu presents all 5 arrow styles (Straight, Elbow, Curved, Dashed, Dual). 5. Click your desired style — the smart connector automatically anchors and aligns!

● TROUBLESHOOTING & TIPS

Frequently Asked Questions & Pro Tips

Solutions to common workflows and tips to get the most out of Lumenboard.

How does Lumenboard save my work?
Lumenboard automatically saves your diagram to browser localStorage after every change (debounced at 500ms). You can safely close or refresh your tab. For permanent backups and sharing across machines, use Export → Project JSON or Lesson ZIP.
How do I adjust which shape is on top of another (Z-index)?
Click the shape you want to adjust. In the right-hand Properties Inspector under "Layers at this coordinate", Lumenboard displays all shapes overlapping that exact location. Choose the target layer and click Place above or Place below.
Why does my PowerPoint (.pptx) download look slightly different?
Lumenboard converts your visual elements into 100% native vector shapes (round rectangles, ellipses, lines, and Aptos fonts) instead of flat screenshots. This ensures every word and box remains editable in PowerPoint.
How do I reset a bent arrow back to automatic routing?
Select the connector arrow, open the right-hand Properties Inspector, and click the Reset automatic route button. The connector will recalculate its optimal path.
Can I use Lumenboard offline?
Yes! Lumenboard runs 100% in your local browser. All code parsing, text analysis, drawing, and PowerPoint generations are executed locally on client side with zero external telemetry.
