Creative Toolkit

The Creation Rail & Freehand Ink Suite

Everything you need for precise diagramming, rapid wireframing, and natural whiteboard sketching.

✎

Freehand Drawing Tools

Transform your canvas into a responsive drawing tablet with natural smoothing:

  • Pencil Tool (✎): Ultra-fine 2px sketching tool for wireframes and quick notes.
  • Pen Tool (✒): Solid ink pen with adjustable stroke width (1px to 24px).
  • Marker Highlighter (▰): Semi-transparent (28% opacity) highlighter that preserves underlying text and shapes.
  • Eraser Tool (◇): Smart stroke eraser that deletes intersected stroke lines upon contact.
  • Color Picker & Opacity Slider: Freely adjust stroke colors and opacity.
↖

Selection & Canvas Navigation

Precision canvas manipulation for small and large-scale diagrams alike:

  • Select Mode (↖): Click single objects, drag selection marquee across multiple items, or hold Shift to add/remove from selection.
  • Pan Tool (✥): Smoothly pan across the infinite canvas without accidentally moving objects.
  • Canvas Auto-Fit & Zoom: Zoom from 35% to 200%. Click the ⌗ button to auto-center and fit the entire diagram in your view.
  • Canvas Area Resize: Expand or trim the white drawing bounds with the −, ＋, and Fit controls.
Intelligent Routing

Smart Connectors & Bridge Hops

Lumenboard features professional circuit-grade routing and intersection collision prevention.

↳

Orthogonal Manhattan Routing

Elbow connectors automatically detect surrounding cards and obstacles on the canvas, computing clean 90-degree orthogonal paths around them.

⌒

Bridge Hops (Bumps)

When two connector lines intersect, Lumenboard automatically renders an elegant 3D bridge arch over the underlying line, eliminating visual ambiguity!

↔

Arrowheads & Line Styles

Easily toggle between Straight, Curved (Bézier), Elbow, and Dashed lines with single-headed (→), bi-directional (↔), or plain (―) line endings.

Full In-Place Text Formatting

Select any text box or press T to unlock word processor capabilities:

  • Typography: Choose from Arial, Georgia, Verdana, Tahoma, or Courier New monospace.
  • Sizing: 7 distinct text sizes from 10px captions to 48px display titles.
  • Inline Styling: Bold (Ctrl+B), Italic (Ctrl+I), Underline (Ctrl+U), Strikethrough.
  • Colors & Highlights: Foreground text color picker and background highlighter swatch.
  • Paragraphs & Lists: Bullet lists, Numbered lists, Indent/Outdent, Left/Center/Right alignment.
▦

Dynamic Interactive Tables

Click the ▦ Table button on the formatting toolbar to open an interactive 8x8 cell selector:

  • Visual Matrix Picker: Hover over the grid to choose any configuration from 1x1 up to 8x8.
  • Formatted Cells: Every cell supports rich text, bold headers, colors, and line breaks.
  • Full Export Fidelity: Tables export seamlessly to native PowerPoint tables, clean SVG nodes, and PDFs!
  • Card & Text Fill: Toggle between opaque color fills and fully transparent background containers.

Automated Diagrams

AI & Smart Visualizers (Code & Text to Diagram)

Instantly turn raw code or long unstructured text into fully connected, editable visual diagrams.

</>

Code Visualizer

Paste HTML, JavaScript, TypeScript, CSS, or backend code. Lumenboard parses the components, functions, and flow:

  • High Level (HLD): Generates an architectural overview showing high-level subsystems, services, and APIs.
  • Low Level (LLD): Generates detailed node-level flows showing individual function calls, event handlers, and data transformations.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your code never leaves your browser and is never executed.
TXT

Text-to-Diagram Engine

Paste notes, process descriptions, meeting minutes, or educational lesson steps:

  • Concept Overview (HLD): Extracts main topics and contextual relationships into cards.
  • Detailed Step Flow (LLD): Breaks down paragraphs and bullet points into sequenced steps with directed arrows.
  • Fully Editable Output: Generated boxes and arrows can be moved, recolored, and restructured immediately.
