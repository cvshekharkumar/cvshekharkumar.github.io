60-Second Quickstart

Lumenboard is a 100% private, client-side, zero-install visual canvas designed for educators, engineers, designers, and students. Get up and running in under a minute:

1

Add Your First Concept Box or Frame

Click the □ Concept or ⌗ Frame icon on the left rail (or click anywhere on the canvas). Choose a basic shape (Rectangle, Pill, Diamond, Circle) from the left library panel.

2

Sketch or Write Freehand with Smooth Pen / Pencil

Select the Pencil (fine 2px sketch), Pen (smooth 4px line), or Marker (highlighter) from the left toolbar. Draw naturally on the whiteboard using your mouse, stylus, or trackpad.

3

Link Elements with Smart Orthogonal Connectors

Hold Shift and drag from any edge handle of a box to another box. Lumenboard automatically calculates the cleanest orthogonal path, avoiding obstacles and generating neat bridge hops when lines cross.

4

Format Text, Tables & Tone Palettes

Double-click text to format with bold, colors, bullet lists, or insert an interactive NxM table. Switch the tone preset across Indigo, Mint, Coral, or Sun with 1-click.

5

Export Anywhere or Present Live

Click Export ↗ in the top right to download high-res PNG, crisp SVG, multi-page PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx), or complete interactive website packages. Hit F11 or click ⛶ Fullscreen for live classroom teaching!

💡
Pro-Tip: Everything is preserved safely in your local browser storage. Press Ctrl+S at any time to checkpoint your project or export a timestamped .json / .zip bundle!

Smart Connectors & Line Crossing Hops

Connecting diagrams has never been cleaner. Lumenboard features intelligent orthogonal routing and automatic bridge hops:

⚡
Auto-Obstacle Avoidance: Elbow connectors automatically route around intervening boxes and nodes, keeping your architectural diagrams crisp and legible.

To connect two nodes: Hover over any shape's perimeter until the handle appears, or hold Shift and drag directly from one box to another. The link locks firmly to the target edge.

✨
Zero Ambiguity with Automatic Bumps: When two connector lines cross over each other on the canvas, Lumenboard automatically renders a smooth curved bridge arc (bump/hop) so viewers instantly know which line leads where!

No manual splitting or fiddling required — the geometric engine calculates segment intersections dynamically and draws the hop in real-time.

Straight Lines

Direct point-to-point connections with custom bend points.

Curved Bezier

Organic smooth curves for mind-maps and user journeys.

Dashed & Arrowheads

Toggle dashed borders and dual start/end arrowheads on or off.

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my project data stored?
All data is stored locally in your browser’s secure IndexedDB and LocalStorage. Lumenboard does not upload your diagrams to external cloud servers unless you explicitly share or export files, ensuring complete privacy.
How do I back up my entire workspace?
Click Export ↗ and select Lesson ZIP or Project JSON. This creates a portable bundle containing all your lessons, layout coordinates, drawings, and embedded media assets that you can restore anytime via File > Import JSON or ZIP.
How does video conversion work without an external server?
Lumenboard runs client-side WebAssembly (FFmpeg.wasm). When you import a video (AVI, MP4, 3GP), your browser's local CPU processes and encodes the video into a fast, streamable WebM format without transferring any video data over the network.
Can I use a stylus or graphics tablet (Wacom, Apple Pencil, Surface)?
Yes! Lumenboard's drawing canvas fully supports pointer events, trackpads, and active styluses with low latency vector stroke smoothing.
How do I combine multiple projects into one?
Use File > Append ZIP. This appends the incoming ZIP's lessons to your existing lesson tabs without overwriting your current work.
