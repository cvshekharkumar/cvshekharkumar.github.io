FAST ONBOARDING

🚀 60-Second Quickstart Guide

Create your very first professional interactive diagram in four simple steps.

1

Add Concepts or Shapes

Click any shape from the Design Library (Rectangle, Circle, Diamond, Decision, Pill) or press □ on the left tool rail to drop a card directly onto the canvas.

  • ✓ Double-click any shape to rename its title and description.
  • ✓ Drag the corners (nw, ne, sw, se) to resize.
2

Connect with Smart Arrows

Hold Shift and hover over any box edge to reveal the + connector handle. Drag the line to any other box to form an automatic orthogonal route!

  • ✓ Automatically avoids obstacles with smart routing.
  • ✓ Overlapping lines automatically create 3D jump bridge bumps.
3

Style, Annotate & Sketch

Select any item to open the Properties Inspector on the right. Customize fills, transparency, 15° rotation snap, and add freehand sketches using the Pencil, Pen, or Highlighter.

  • ✓ Add formatted rich-text blocks with live tables (▦ Table).
  • ✓ Customize layer order with "Place above" / "Place below".
4

Export or Present Anywhere

Click Export ↗ at the top right to download an Editable PowerPoint (.pptx), high-res PNG, crisp SVG, multi-page PDF, or complete project JSON.

  • ✓ Every lesson tab exports to a dedicated native PPT slide.
  • ✓ All changes auto-save in real time to your local storage.
💡
Local-First Auto-Save Guarantee

Lumenboard automatically saves all your project changes locally in your browser. You can refresh or close your browser tab without losing any progress!

APPLICATION LAYOUT

🖥️ Interface Anatomy & Workspace Zones

Get familiar with the studio environment and tools available at your fingertips.

🧭 Interactive Workspace Explorer

1. Top Navigation Bar

Houses global project utilities, menus, and rapid generators:

  • Brand & Save Indicator: Displays live save status ("Saved locally" vs "Saving…").
  • File Menu: Import single JSON projects, multi-lesson ZIP files, DOCX lesson packs, or external images, icons, and animated GIFs.
  • Templates: Quick-start with pre-configured architectural structures (Process flow, DOM tree, System map, Lesson canvas).
  • Visualize Code: AI/AST generator converting raw HTML/CSS/JS code into structured HLD or LLD diagrams.
  • Visualize Text: Natural language diagrammer that converts paragraphs and lesson notes into step-by-step concept flowcharts.
  • Fullscreen Toggle (Ctrl + F): Maximizes your canvas area for immersive presentation and designing.
  • Dark / Light Theme Toggle: Effortlessly switch between warm cream paper mode and sleek dark mode.
  • Export Center (↗): Export to Editable PPTX, PDF, SVG, PNG, ZIP, and JSON.

2. Creation Rail (Left Vertical Bar)

Your primary toolkit for selecting, manipulating, and authoring content:

  • Select Tool (↖): Move objects, drag bounding marquee selection boxes, resize, and rotate.
  • Pan Tool (✥): Grab and pan freely across the expansive infinite-canvas working area.
  • Pencil Tool (✎): Soft 72% opacity sketch stroke for notes and wireframing.
  • Pen Tool (✒): Crisp 100% opaque solid ink stroke for precision drawing.
  • Marker Highlighter (▰): 28% translucent highlighter to emphasize diagram areas.
  • Eraser Tool (◇): Precision circular stroke eraser to clean up sketches.
  • Add Concept Card (□): Drops an interactive learning card onto the board.
  • Add Decision Diamond (◇): Drops a flowchart branching decision node.
  • Write Formatted Text (T): Click anywhere on canvas to create a rich-text card with full WYSIWYG formatting and table support.
  • Add Sticky Note / Callout (▰): Add yellow insight notes and teaching callouts.
  • Add Lesson Frame (⌗): Groups related diagram cards together into structured boundary frames.

3. Design & Templates Library (Collapsible Sidebar)

Browse, filter, and drop items directly onto your canvas:

  • Live Search: Instant fuzzy search across shapes, arrows, desktop icons, and learning blocks.
  • Basic Shapes: Rectangle, Square, Circle, Diamond, Decision hexagon, Pill (Start/End terminal), and Callout notes.
  • Arrow Palette: Straight, Straight Line, Both-sided (↔), Elbow / Orthogonal (↳), Curved bezier (∿), and Dashed (⇢).
  • Desktop Icons: Pre-styled technology and workflow badges (`WEB`, `PC`, `DB`, `API`, ``, `DOC`, `USR`, `CFG`).
  • Collapse Button (‹ / ›): Hide the sidebar anytime to expand your working canvas view.

4. Main Working Canvas Stage

The core design surface where diagrams come to life:

  • Zooming & Scrolling: Zoom smoothly from 35% up to 200% with mouse wheel (Ctrl + Wheel) or zoom buttons.
  • Configurable Canvas Dimensions: Adjust your working surface size (2400 × 1600 by default) or click Fit to tightly bound your diagram.
  • Multi-Select Marquee: Click and drag on empty canvas to select multiple items at once and move or delete them together.
  • 8-Point Resize & Free Rotation: Drag corner handles or use the top rotation peg (Hold Shift to snap in 15° increments).

5. Properties Inspector (Right Sidebar)

Context-sensitive panel displaying precise controls for whatever is currently selected:

  • Content Editing: Title, subtitle detail, and short badge text.
  • Color Palette & Custom Hex Picker: Choose from 12 curated pastel tones or pick any custom RGB/Hex color.
  • Fill Mode (Opaque vs Transparent): Make cards or frames transparent to reveal underlying images or backgrounds.
  • Layer Ordering: Dynamically reorder overlapping objects at the exact coordinate ("Place above" / "Place below").
  • Geometric Dimensions: Precision numeric inputs for X, Y, Width, and Height.
  • Connector Customization: Arrow styles, stroke thickness (1px–10px), line opacity, and line ending types.

6. Lesson Bar & Multi-Slide Manager

Manage multi-step lessons and presentation slides:

  • Lesson Tabs: Switch between lessons with a single click.
  • Tab Color Badges: Right-click any tab to assign an accent color.
  • Rename Lessons: Double-click tab or click the pencil icon to rename.
  • Add Lessons (＋): Create infinite sequential slides for your course or presentation deck.
  • Independent Undo/Redo: Each lesson tab maintains its own independent 250-step history stack!

HOW-TO RECIPES & FAQ

💡 Frequently Asked Questions & Step-by-Step Recipes

Solutions for common workflows and advanced diagramming techniques.

How do I create a decision tree with branching logic? ▼

1. Drop a Decision shape (◇) from the Design Library onto your canvas.

2. Double-click to set your conditional question (e.g. "Is User Authenticated?").

3. Drop two target cards below (e.g. "Redirect to Dashboard" and "Show Login Screen").

4. Hold Shift and drag from the left/right edges of the Decision diamond to connect them with labeled arrows.

How does grouping with Lesson Frames work? ▼

Click Add frame (⌗) from the rail to create a boundary frame. When you place shapes or notes inside a frame, moving the frame moves all contained elements together as a unified group!

Can I edit my exported PowerPoint presentation? ▼

Yes! Lumenboard exports true native PowerPoint shapes and vector text rather than flat images. In Microsoft PowerPoint, you can click into any shape, change colors, edit fonts, and modify layout dynamically.

How do I adjust overlapping layers (Z-Index)? ▼

Select any object that overlaps another. In the right Properties Inspector under "Layers at this coordinate", pick the target overlapping object and click Place above or Place below.
