2. Quickstart in 60 Seconds

Get up and running with your first interactive board in three simple steps:

Step 1

Pick a Shape or Tool

Click any item from the left library (Card, Note, Decision, Frame) or select a drawing tool like Pen or Pencil from the top rail.

Step 2

Connect & Customize

Hold Shift and drag from any box to another to create an auto-routed arrow. Use the right Inspector panel to tweak colors, code, and text.

Step 3

Export or Present

Press Ctrl + F for distraction-free fullscreen presentation mode, or export as crisp PNG, SVG vector, or Word document from the File Menu.

💡 Pro-Tip: Deep Undo History

Lumenboard keeps track of up to 250 undo steps! You can fearlessly experiment knowing you can always press Ctrl + Z to undo and Ctrl + Y to redo.

6. Smart Connectors & Jump Bumps

Connecting nodes in Lumenboard is effortless and intelligent. Lines adapt to your layout, avoid overlapping cards, and prevent visual ambiguity.

⚡ The Fast Shift-Drag Gesture

1. Hover over any diagram node.
2. Hold the Shift key — a blue + connector handle will illuminate on the card edge.
3. Click and drag the handle directly onto the target card and release!
4. Pick your desired arrow type from the pop-up menu (Straight, Elbow, Curved, Dashed).

1. Straight Line (―)

Direct shortest-path connection between two nodes. Best for clean, minimal diagrams.

2. Elbow / Orthogonal (↳)

Right-angle 90° bends that route neatly around cards. Perfect for architecture schemas.

3. Smooth Curved (∿)

Organic cubic Bezier arc that creates soft, readable connections across wide layouts.

4. Dashed Route (⇢)

Semi-transparent dashed stroke for asynchronous flows, optional dependencies, or loose associations.

🌉 Automatic Bridge Jump Bumps

When two connector lines cross over each other on the canvas, Lumenboard automatically calculates their geometric intersection point and renders a smooth semi-circular arch (bridge bump) on the upper line. This prevents viewers from mistaking crossing lines for a 4-way junction!

Unidirectional (→)

Standard flow arrow directing the viewer from source to target.

Bidirectional (↔)

Arrows on both ends for two-way synchronization or duplex communication.

Headless Line (―)

Plain line with no arrowheads for structural hierarchies or mind-maps.

7. Rich Text Formatting & Tables

Lumenboard contains a full WYSIWYG rich text engine directly inside every node, card, and floating text box.

Styling & Hierarchy

Apply Bold (Ctrl+B), Italics (Ctrl+I), Underline (Ctrl+U), Strikethrough, custom font colors, and bright yellow/mint background highlights.

Lists & Indentation

Create organized bulleted lists, numbered step sequences, and adjust multi-level indentations using the formatting toolbar buttons.

Format Painter

Click the Copy Format button to clone font weights, colors, and background highlights from one text block directly to another.

Grid Table Generator

Click the Insert Table icon to open a visual grid picker (e.g. 3 × 3). Insert interactive HTML tables into cards to present matrix data or comparisons.
