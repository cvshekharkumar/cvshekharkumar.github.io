01. Overview & Architecture

Core Concept

Lumenboard is a high-performance visual whiteboard, teaching studio, and diagramming platform built specifically for developers, teachers, system designers, and content creators. It bridges the gap between precise vector diagramming (like Draw.io/Lucidchart) and organic handwritten explanation boards (like Miro/Excalidraw).

🔒

100% Private & Local

Your diagrams, code, and text notes never leave your computer. All state is automatically persisted in browser LocalStorage and high-capacity IndexedDB.

🎯

Multi-Lesson Tabs

Organize complex courses and multi-tier systems into numbered pages. Drag-to-reorder tabs, assign custom tab colors, and search across hundreds of pages effortlessly.

✨

Smart Visualizers

Instantly convert raw code (HTML, JS, Python, SQL) or plain English process text into clean, connected HLD (High-Level) or LLD (Low-Level) flowcharts with one click.

03. 2-Minute Quick Start

Interactive Workflow
01

Add Concepts, Shapes or Visualizers

Click any shape in the Left Design Dock (Rectangle, Square, Circle, Diamond, Decision, Pill) or click Visualize Text / Code in the top menu to auto-generate a diagram from your thoughts.

💡

Pro Tip: Last Pointer Position

When you click any item in the library, Lumenboard places it precisely where your cursor last hovered over the canvas!

02

Connect with Smart Arrows

Hover over any shape edge until the purple ＋ handle appears. Drag from the handle directly to another shape. Lumenboard will pop open an instant connector menu to choose Straight, Elbow, Curved, or Dashed arrows!

⚡

Shift-Drag Superpower

Hold Shift and drag from any shape or arrow endpoint to instantly spawn a new linked connection.

03

Write, Highlight & Annotate

Double-click any text box to open the full rich-text editor toolbar. Choose font sizes, bold/italics, bullet lists, or click ▦ Table to insert a table grid. Pick the Pen (✒) or Marker Highlighter (▰) to draw over concepts.

04

Export in Any Format

Click the Export ↗ button in the top right. Download your work as high-res PNG, lossless vector SVG, multi-page PDF, a timestamped Lesson ZIP, or a standalone swipeable Lesson HTML file.

05. Pen, Pencil & Annotation Suite

Freehand Engine

Lumenboard includes a built-in vector smoothing freehand engine that feels like real ink on paper while generating lightweight SVG paths:

✎

Pencil & Pen Tools

Pencil (✎): Fine 1–8px sketch strokes ideal for wireframing and subtle marginal notes.

Pen (✒): Smooth, weighted vector strokes (1–24px) for bold handwritten titles, formulas, and arrows.

▰

Marker Highlighter (28% Translucent)

Soft 10–50px wide highlighting strokes with calibrated 28% transparency. Allows underlying text, code boxes, and diagrams to shine through crisply without visual distortion.

◇

Dynamic Eraser

Adjustable 10–100px circular eraser that effortlessly deletes freehand strokes when brushing over them. Non-destructive and fully registered in the undo/redo stack (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).

🖊

Drawing Tablet & Stylus Support

Engineered with dedicated event listeners for drawing tablets (Wacom, Huion, Apple Pencil). Right-click and barrel buttons are automatically prevented from disrupting active strokes.

07. Smart Connectors & Automatic Routing

Smart Lines

Arrows in Lumenboard aren't just dumb lines—they possess intelligent geometry calculation, obstacle avoidance, automatic crossing bridges, and manual waypoint manipulation:

↳

6 Versatile Connector Types

  • Straight Line (―): Clean linear dividers without arrowheads.
  • Straight Arrow (→): Direct point-to-point flow vectors.
  • Both Sides (↔): Bidirectional relationships & sync channels.
  • Orthogonal Elbow (↳): 90-degree stepped architectural bus routing.
  • Curved Bézier (∿): Organic flow lines with customizable inflection.
  • Dashed Line (⇢): Dotted asynchronous or optional paths.
🌉

Automatic Line-Crossing Bridge Bumps

When two orthogonal or straight connectors cross each other on the canvas, Lumenboard calculates the intersection and automatically renders an elegant arch / bridge bump over the lower line so your diagrams look like professional electrical schematics!

14. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Help & FAQ
Your diagrams are stored 100% locally on your computer inside your browser's LocalStorage and IndexedDB databases. No diagrams or media files are ever transmitted to any remote cloud server. Even if you accidentally close your tab or browser, everything is automatically restored when you reopen the app.
Go to File → Import PDF as lessons (or simply drag and drop your .pdf file anywhere onto the canvas). Lumenboard will read the document and automatically create one lesson tab for each PDF page!
Whenever two connector lines cross over each other on the canvas, Lumenboard evaluates their z-index layer and drawing order. The connector on the upper layer automatically draws a smooth white bridge mask with an arched bump over the lower line, preventing confusing visual intersections.
Click the File menu and select "Remove browser saved data". This safely clears the local diagram cache and media database, resetting Lumenboard to its pristine initial state.
