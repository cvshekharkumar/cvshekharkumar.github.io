🚀 Get Up to Speed Fast

The 60-Second Quick Start

Follow this frictionless 6-step blueprint to build your first high-impact visual diagram in under a minute.

1

Choose a Starting Point

Click Templates in the top menu or left sidebar to pick a pre-engineered layout (Web foundations, Architecture, Flowcharts), or click ＋ on the bottom bar for a fresh canvas.

2

Drop Shapes & Concept Cards

Click any shape in the left Design Library or quick-add icons (Concept □, Decision ◇, Frame ⌗). Double click any item on canvas to type directly inside it.

3

Connect Nodes with Smart Arrows

Hover over any shape's perimeter to reveal connection points. Drag an arrow to another shape. Choose Straight, Elbow, Curved, or Dashed. Notice how overlapping lines automatically generate 3D bridge arches!

4

Style with Harmonious Tones & Formats

Select any item to open the right Properties Inspector. Switch tone palettes (Indigo, Mint, Coral, Sun), tweak opacity sliders, or insert tables and code snippets.

5

Organize Multi-Page Lessons

Add consecutive slides/pages along the bottom bar. Double-click page tabs to rename them, and drag-and-drop to reorder lessons dynamically.

6

Export Anywhere

Click the top-right Export button to generate high-resolution PNG images, multi-page vector PDFs, native editable PowerPoint (.pptx) slides, or offline lesson ZIP packages.

❓ Got Questions?

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Instant solutions for common workflow questions, performance tips, and file handling:

How do I recover pages if I accidentally delete them in bulk? ▼
Don't worry! Whenever you delete pages using the Delete Pages panel, a persistent Undo Deletion notification banner appears immediately. Click it to restore all deleted pages with their items, connections, and ordering intact. You can also press Ctrl+Z to undo canvas actions.
Can I use Lumenboard completely offline? ▼
Yes, 100%! Lumenboard is designed as a local-first application. All diagram generation, PDF parsing, PPTX compilation, and canvas rendering happen directly inside your browser without sending sensitive diagram data to remote servers. Export your work as a Lumenboard ZIP for a complete offline backup.
How do I ensure connector lines don't look tangled in complex diagrams? ▼
Lumenboard includes two built-in solutions: (1) Automatic Jump Arches that render 3D bridge arcs whenever lines cross, and (2) Orthogonal Elbow Style which routes connections in crisp 90-degree steps. You can also drag the midpoint diamond handles on connectors to customize bend paths manually.
What is the difference between "Import JSON or ZIP" and "Append ZIP"? ▼
Import JSON or ZIP replaces your current workspace with the imported project, ideal when switching to a different curriculum or document. Append ZIP adds all imported lesson slides to your existing page list without removing any of your current work.
How do I copy formatting from one styled card to multiple other cards? ▼
Select the formatted card or text block, click the Format Painter (paintbrush) icon in the toolbar, and then click on any destination card or text element. The font, size, weight, color, and background styling will be applied instantly.
How do I import large multi-page PDF documents without freezing? ▼
Lumenboard uses a dedicated Web Worker stream via pdfjs-dist. When you drop or import a PDF, a real-time progress monitor shows page-by-page progress. Each PDF page is automatically converted into its own clean lesson slide.
