Selection, Grouping & 8-Point Transformation

Manipulate whiteboard elements with precision desktop-grade bounding box controls.

Multi-Object Marquee Selection

  • Marquee Lasso Drag: Switch to Select tool (V), then click and drag across the empty canvas to draw an indigo bounding box around multiple objects.
  • Unified Group Shifting: Click and drag anywhere inside the selection box to reposition all highlighted elements simultaneously.
  • Ctrl / Shift + Click Toggle: Hold Ctrl or Shift while clicking elements to add or deselect individual items without resetting your entire selection.
  • Instant Group Erasure: Hit Delete or Backspace to remove all selected items in one keystroke.

8-Point Handle Resizing & In-Place Direct Editing

  • 8 Transform Handles: Drag top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right, or edge handles to scale shapes, images, cards, and diagrams freely.
  • Direct In-Place Editing: Single-tap Text tool (T) or double-tap any existing text box or sticky note (S) to edit directly in-place with multi-line auto-expansion and click-word cursor positioning.
  • Mobile Virtual Keyboard Auto-Pan: When typing on phones and tablets, the camera automatically glides the whiteboard screen upward so your active text stays comfortably visible above the virtual keyboard without being covered.
  • Toolbar Auto-Dimming: During inline text editing on mobile, overlapping toolbars auto-dim to ensure 100% unobstructed readability while typing.
  • Clipboard Media Pasting: Paste any diagram, screenshot, or animated GIF directly onto the canvas using Ctrl+V or drag-and-drop.

Then simply click or tap on any other box to immediately replace its content with the copied text!

Slide Decks, Presentations & Export Capabilities

Multi-page lectures, camera viewport navigation, spotlight mode, and standalone HTML bundle generation.

Slide Deck Manager & Viewport Controls

  • Add Page: Click + Add Page in the bottom dock to append a fresh slide to your lecture deck.
  • Auto-Center & Slide Highlight: Adding a new slide or clicking Next/Previous automatically centers the active slide in the bottom thumbnail carousel with a distinct glowing highlight ring.
  • Rename Slide (Double-Click): Double-click (or double-tap on touchscreens) any slide thumbnail at the bottom to rename it (e.g. "Introduction", "Theorem Proof"). Press Enter to save.
  • Swap & Reorder Slides: Drag and drop any slide thumbnail directly in the bottom carousel to swap/reorder slides effortlessly. You can also click the left/right arrow buttons (◀ / ▶) in the bottom dock or press Alt + ← / →.
  • Clear Slide / Delete Page: Click the trash icon in the top actions bar to clear all objects on the current slide, or click the ✕ icon in the bottom dock to delete the active slide.
  • Duplicate Slide: Click the copy icon to duplicate the active slide and all its visual contents.
  • Slide Switcher: Click any thumbnail in the bottom carousel to navigate instantly between lecture pages.
  • Pan Canvas: Select Hand Tool (Hand) or hold Space while dragging to pan across an infinite canvas.
  • Spotlight Mode: Click the Sun icon in the top header to dim the background and focus a movable bright spotlight on key formulas during live lecturing.

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Answers to common queries regarding local storage, offline mode, privacy, and touchscreens.

Does LearnBoard Studio work offline without internet?

Yes! All drawing tools, geometric shapes, template generation, image rendering, multi-slide management, and local storage state caching run 100% offline in your browser. Live Google AI knowledge retrieval and AI quiz generation require an active network connection to query live references.

Where is my whiteboard drawing data stored?

All data is saved locally on your device in your browser's localStorage. Zero drawing telemetry is sent to any remote database. Clicking Clear Cookies in the top bar completely cleans all cached data and resets the board to blank state.

How do I export my lecture as a standalone multi-slide presentation?

Click Export > Export HTML (All Pages) in the top navigation. The app bundles all your slides into an ultra-clean standalone .html file with responsive presentation controls that you can email to students or present on any smart projector without needing any software installation.

How do I cross out or mark completed tasks and cut items?

Select any text or sticky note and click the Strikethrough button (S̶) in the top toolbar or modal chip to draw a clean strikethrough line across the text (or use markdown syntax ~~completed task~~). To remove a completed item while copying it to your clipboard, press Ctrl+X or tap Cut / Done.

How do I change text color versus background fill color?

In the top properties bar (Row 1), toggle the Color Target Switcher between 🖊️ Text / Pen (to style ink strokes, pencil lines, and font typography) and 🪣 Background (to style shape interiors and sticky note fills). Click the Transparent Fill (🚫) button to make shape bodies transparent without losing border strokes.

How do I format bullet lists, numbers, and clone styles with Format Painter?

Use the formatting buttons in Row 2 of the top toolbar or inline editor chips: tap • to toggle bullet points, 1. to toggle numbered lists, and A- / A+ to scale font size. To duplicate styling across items, select the styled object, click Format Painter (🖌️) or press Ctrl+Shift+C, then click any target shape or text to apply.

How does mobile typing and virtual keyboard auto-shift work?

On smartphones and tablets, typing is ultra-smooth and frictionless:

  • Virtual Keyboard Auto-Shift Up: When your device's on-screen keyboard pops up, the whiteboard camera automatically glides the screen upward in real-time so your active text stays comfortably visible in the upper viewing zone without being covered by the keyboard.
  • Toolbar Auto-Dimming: During in-place text editing, top and left docks auto-dim (opacity-15) so toolbars never overlap or obstruct your reading.
  • Auto Size & Multi-line: Text boxes auto-expand in width and height dynamically as you type, and auto-focus with accurate cursor positioning.
How do I export my whiteboard lecture as a Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) presentation?

In the top bar, open the Export dropdown (or open the mobile menu) and select 📊 Export PowerPoint (.pptx). The app automatically bundles all slides in your deck into a standard 16:9 widescreen .pptx presentation file. You can open and present this file in Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, Apple Keynote, or upload it directly to your school's LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Google Classroom).

How does the AI Tutor's Hinglish Translation and Whiteboard Recommendation work?

When you query the AI Tutor for any derivation, scientific explanation, or solution, you can click 🇮🇳 Translate to Hinglish. The engine converts the academic reasoning into conversational Hindi written in the English/Roman alphabet while keeping formulas, variables, and technical terms intact.

Once translated, the system displays a live Recommendation Banner recommending immediate insertion onto the canvas. Clicking 📋 Insert Hinglish Solution Directly onto Whiteboard instantly places a styled amber notes card at the center of your whiteboard screen, auto-saves to memory, and triggers confirmation audio.

How do I convert an entire whiteboard sheet into Hinglish and export it in that language?

In the top navigation bar (or mobile menu), select 🇮🇳 Hinglish (or any regional/international language) from the Sheet Language Selector and click the ON/OFF Toggle switch.

  • Instant Conversion: All text boxes, sticky notes, cards, and interactive quiz elements on the active sheet instantly convert into conversational Hinglish while preserving formulas, numbers, and variables.
  • Automatic Export in Hinglish: Any export you generate (Export PNG, JPEG, PowerPoint .pptx, or standalone .html) is saved directly in the translated language!
  • Non-Destructive Toggle: Clicking the toggle back to OFF instantly restores the original English text without losing any formatting or drawing strokes.
Does LearnBoard Studio support stylus pens, Apple Pencil, and touchscreens?

Yes! The canvas engine utilizes native W3C Pointer Events with sub-pixel resolution and velocity-adaptive Bezier smoothing, making it fully compatible with stylus pens, Wacom tablets, iPads, Surface pens, and touch smartboards.
